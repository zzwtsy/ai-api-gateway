# Decision supersession

Search for the same mechanism, rejected alternatives, and old filenames before changing a Decision:

- Keep an `implemented` Decision while it still constrains current engineering.
- When a new Decision fully replaces the old one, the old Note may move to `superseded/` and link the new owner.
- When an independent compatibility obligation, durable format, current behavior, or rejection rationale remains, classify the relationship as partial and preserve cross-links.
- Never rewrite an old Decision silently into the opposite conclusion.
- Current Conventions, source, and tests own reality; Decisions own durable rationale.

Do not expand a local cleanup into repository-wide Decision archival. Revisit a Decision only when the behavioral boundary or durable rationale changes.
